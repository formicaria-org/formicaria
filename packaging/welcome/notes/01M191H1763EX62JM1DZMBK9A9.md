# Start here

This is your notebook. Every note is a plain text file in the **vault** folder next to
the app — you can open them in any editor, and copying that folder takes your notes
with it. Nothing lives anywhere else.

## Writing something

Press **+** and choose **New note**. There is no folder to pick and no title to invent
first. (Or just press **c**. **/** jumps to the search box.)

To change a note later, **double-click** it. While you are editing, typing **/** offers
to link another note or drop in a file.

## Finding it again

Your notes are never filed away in one place. Instead you look at the same notes in
different ways. The strip of buttons at the side of the window lists them — pick one:

- **Timeline** — everything, by the day you wrote it. Good place to start.
- **Board** — cards in columns. Drag a card to move it; the column is just a property.
- **Agenda** — a calendar and a list, for anything you gave a date.
- **Search** — a word anywhere in any note. Also the magnifier in the strip.

There are a few more in the list. A note appears in whichever of these fits it. It is
not moved, copied, or filed.

## Giving a note a shape

Double-click a note to edit it, then use the **+** in its header to add a title, tags, a
status, or dates. A status puts it on the Board; a date puts it on the Agenda. None of
it is required — a note with nothing but words is a perfectly good note.

## Not losing it

**Back up**, in that same strip of buttons, records the history of your notes and can
send a copy somewhere else you own. Photos, PDFs and recordings are larger, so they are a separate, optional
step in the same place.

## Where the rest is

In the strip too, after Back up:

- **Help** — the whole manual, inside the app, on a wide window. No internet needed;
  on a narrow one, open `Manual.html` from the folder you unpacked.
- **Settings** — the gear: appearance, layout, and what each notebook is set up to do.

You can delete this note whenever you like. Nothing depends on it.
