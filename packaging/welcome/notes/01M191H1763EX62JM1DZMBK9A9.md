# Start here

This is your notebook. Every note is a plain text file in the **vault** folder next to
the app — you can open them in any editor, and copying that folder takes your notes
with it. Nothing lives anywhere else.

## Writing something

Press **+** and choose **New note**. There is no folder to pick and no title to invent
first. (**Ctrl+K** opens a search box over the same commands, once you know their names.)

To change a note later, **double-click** it. While you are editing, typing **/** offers
to link another note or drop in a file.

## Finding it again

Your notes are never filed away in one place. Instead you look at the same notes in
different ways. At the top of every window is the name of the way you are looking now —
**click it** to change to the next one (or scroll the wheel anywhere along that bar):

- **Timeline** — everything, by the day you wrote it. Good place to start.
- **Board** — cards in columns. Drag a card to move it; the column is just a property.
- **Agenda** — a calendar and a list, for anything you gave a date.
- **Search** — a word anywhere in any note. Also the magnifier in the top bar.

There are a few more in the ring — keep clicking. A note appears in whichever of these
fits it. It is not moved, copied, or filed.

## Giving a note a shape

Open a note and press **Edit** to add a title, tags, a status, or dates. A status puts
it on the Board; a date puts it on the Agenda. None of it is required — a note with
nothing but words is a perfectly good note.

## Not losing it

**Back up** (top right) records the history of your notes, and can send a copy somewhere
else you own. Photos, PDFs and recordings are larger, so they are a separate, optional
step in the same place.

## Where the rest is

At the right of the top bar, after Back up:

- **Help** — the whole manual, inside the app. No internet needed.
- **Settings** — the gear: appearance, layout, and what each notebook is set up to do.

You can delete this note whenever you like. Nothing depends on it.
