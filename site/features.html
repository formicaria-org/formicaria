What you can do

  • Notes

    Write in Markdown, one note per file. The editor shows the result as you write, and the text is still the file. Search looks inside every note. A Logseq or Obsidian folder can be brought in, with its links kept.

    • Notes
    • Importing

  • Tasks and a calendar

    A task is a note with a date on it. Give a note a status or a date and the same notes become a board, or a month and week calendar.

    • Views

  • Reminders

    A note with a start or a due date reminds you at its time, once you turn that on in Settings.

    • Reminders

  • A food log

    Log what you eat from a list of 18,300 foods that comes with the app and is searched offline. Keep recipes, plan a menu with a shopping list, and follow a diet from a library of sourced ones or set your own targets.

    • Food and training

  • A training log

    A timetable for the week, 117 exercise cards in the words of the body that published each, and training regimes to follow, each with a week to start from.

    • Fitness

  • Backup, and working with someone else

    Send a notebook somewhere else to keep it safe, and see how a note looked last week. Two people can keep the same notebook in step, each on their own machine; if both edit the same note, their changes are merged rather than making you choose.

    • Backup
    • Collaboration

  • An assistant on your own device

    Optional, and off until you turn it on. A small AI model that runs on your own device answers in a note's discussion, can look something up on the web, and drafts edits for you to approve. No account and no key; your notes never leave the machine.

    • The study assistant

Food and fitness give no advice: every figure names the body that published it. They can be hidden in Settings if you want only a notebook. History and backup need the program git on Linux and macOS (Windows carries its own), and the assistant downloads its model the first time you turn it on. When something optional is missing, the app says so and nothing else is affected.

formicaria is the plural of formicarium, an ant colony's nest: you can keep several notebooks, each its own place. This site uses no scripts, no cookies and nothing from any other server.
