How far it is proven

Taken from the project's own status notes. What is listed above is what has been built, which is not the same as what has been proven on your machine.

  • Young. The work began on 14 July 2026. This is one person's project, built alongside their research rather than as a product.
  • Used daily on Linux and Android — the two systems the author works on, and the only two anything is checked against.
  • macOS, Windows and iPhone have been run by other people and reported working — the macOS report alongside real faults — but on early versions (0.2 and 0.3), not the current one. Nobody is known to have downloaded anything newer. None of it is verified by the author, who has no Mac, no Windows machine and no iPhone. The builds are made and tested automatically and the downloads are real; that is a different claim from someone having opened one.
  • The newest parts are the least proven. Food and fitness are early and have been used by the author alone. Reminders have never been seen to arrive on a phone, and their notifications on Windows and macOS have never been run.
  • The assistant has been run on Linux and Android. On macOS and Windows it is built but has never been run.
