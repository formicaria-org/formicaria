# See your notes your way

The same notes, shown differently — pick one from the list of views:

- **Shelves** — notes as books. Give a note a **shelf** in its details and it moves there.
- **Timeline** — newest first.
- **Board** — notes as cards by status: to do, doing, done.
- **Agenda** — notes with a date, on a calendar.
