# Ask Myrme

Myrme is a small assistant that runs on this device. Turn it on in **Settings → Study assistant**.

Then, in a note's discussion, write **@Myrme** and what you want. The buttons under the discussion
add the commands — **/recipe** makes a recipe of what you said, **/transcribe** turns a recording
into text. Myrme only ever *proposes*; you accept or change it.
