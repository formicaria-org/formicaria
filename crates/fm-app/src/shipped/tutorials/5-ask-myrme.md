# Ask Myrme

Myrme is a small assistant that runs **on this device**. What you ask it, and the notes it reads,
never leave it.

## Turn it on

Open **Settings → Assistant → Study assistant** and switch it on. It shows each model it can use,
with its download size, and downloads the one you pick. You can keep working while it does.

![Settings, Assistant: the study assistant, off, saying that turning it on downloads a model first
and asks before it does](manual:images/assistant-settings.png)

## Ask it something

Every note has a **Discussion** below its text. Open it, write **@Myrme** and what you want, and
send. Myrme answers in the discussion, signed with the device that answered.

You do not have to remember any commands: the buttons under the discussion add them, each with a
line saying what it does. A few:

- **/transcribe** turns the note's recordings and photos into text.
- **/recipe** makes a recipe of what you said or wrote.
- **/propose** drafts a change to the note.

## You decide

Myrme never changes a note by itself. What it would write comes as a **proposal**: accept it,
change it first, or turn it down.
