# What the trackers may claim, and on whose authority — a source register (2026-10-11)

> **A first pass, and a contract.** The ruling is `decisions.md#data`, 2026-10-11, *nothing is
> invented*. This file is the list that ruling points at: every diet label, target, formula and
> training suggestion the app offers must name a row here. A row that has not been read at its
> source is marked, and **must be read before anything is built on it**.

## The owner's rule

> *"We need not to invent anything but base it on grounded and documented and well reputable
> sources after a proper research."* — on diet labels (Mediterranean, vegetarian, vegan), recipes,
> training regimes and workouts, and any suggestion computed from a person's height, weight or
> other measurements.

## How to read the *Checked* column

- **source** — the source's own page or document was fetched and read on 2026-10-11.
- **search** — taken from a search result or a secondary summary. Not yet read at the source.
- **memory** — from recall. Not looked up. Treat as a lead, not a fact.

Nothing marked *search* or *memory* may be shipped as a number, a definition or a claim.

## 3. Diet labels — what a name means

**A label is only offered with the definition it uses, named.** Where there is no single
definition, the app says which one it is applying.

The paper itself says there is **no standard definition** of "vegetarian". Appropriately planned patterns can be nutritionally adequate for adults | source |

| | Trichopoulou MDS (2003), alternate MED score (Fung 2005) | Research scores; relative cut-offs | memory |

| **DASH** | [NHLBI, DASH eating plan](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/education/dash-eating-plan) | Daily and weekly servings by food group; at 2,000 kcal: grains 6–8, vegetables 4–5, fruit 4–5, low-fat dairy 2–3, meat/poultry/fish 6 or fewer, sodium 2,300 mg. Other calorie levels not read | search |
| **Planetary health diet** | EAT-Lancet Commission (2019; a 2025 update is reported) | Reference intakes in grams per day | memory |

**Consequence for the design.** A diet label cannot be a tag on a person that changes numbers
silently. It is either (a) a set of *excluded food groups*, checkable only if foods carry a
food-group classification the list does not yet have, or (b) a *named screener or serving table*
the person fills in or is measured against, with its source shown.

## 4. Recipes

A list of ingredients is a fact and is not protected; the *wording* of a recipe usually is. So a
recipe collection needs a licence for its text, not only for its idea.

is learned about taste from anywhere else.

## 9. Not done, and needed before any of this is built on

- **Read at the source** everything marked *search* above — first the equations (Mifflin–St Jeor,
  NASEM 2023), the ACSM 2026 paper, the Mediterranean pyramid's serving table and MEDAS's 14 items.
- **The U.S. *Daily Servings by Calorie Level* document** (2025–2030) was not read.
- **Micronutrient reference tables** (DRI, EFSA) were not fetched; nothing here is a table.
- **Food groups.** A vegetarian or vegan check needs each food classified; the list carries no
  such field. FNDDS has food categories (WWEIA) that could supply one — not examined.
- **Licences** for recipe text, the Compendium's tables, and screening questionnaires.
- **Safety.** No source has been chosen for a lowest sensible energy target, for who should not
  be offered a deficit, or for when to say "ask a clinician". Until one is, the app sets no floor
  and offers no deficit of its own.
- **Not medical advice** has to be said somewhere a person sees it; where and how is undecided.
