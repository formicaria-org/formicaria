# Music

formicaria keeps your songs beside your notes. **A song is a note**, like a meal in Food: its title,
artist, album and the rest are the note's details, and its audio file is attached to it like any
other file. Open a song's note and it plays there.

> **New, and only the start.** Built in November 2026 and tried on a computer only. For now you can
> add songs, search them and open them; albums, artists, playlists and a player that keeps playing
> while you move around the app come next.

## Turning it on and off

**Music** is one of the app's [groups](./views.md#the-groups), beside Food, Fitness and Health. It
opens on **Songs**. It is shown unless you hide it, with the same switch as the others:

**Settings → Food & reminders → Food and training → Show Food, Fitness, Health and Music on this device.**

## Adding a song

Press the **＋** in Music, then **Choose files** — one song or many — or drop the files onto the
sheet on a computer.

- **Each song fills itself in from its own file.** Most music files carry their details inside them:
  the title, artist, album, track number, year, genre, length and often the cover. formicaria reads
  them on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **A file with no details** is read by its name: `05 - Some Artist - Some Title.mp3` gives the
  artist *Some Artist* and the title *Some Title*.
- **One file** shows its form: change anything before you press **Save**.
- **Several files** show one line each. Press a line to change it, then **Save** them all at once.
- **A song you already have is not added twice.** If a file's audio is already one of your songs,
  its line says so and it is left out.

What you type is kept in the song's note. **The file itself is never changed.** The details its
file carried are kept on the file's own note too, so you can always see what it said.

Undo (**↶**) takes back the songs you just saved (a very large batch, 200 songs at a time).

## Songs

The search comes first: type any part of a title, an artist, an album or a genre. Every word you
type must match, capitals and accents don't matter (*beyonce* finds *Beyoncé*), and the list
narrows as you type. Press a song to open its note.

## Where your songs are kept

A song's file is kept in your notebook like any picture or PDF you attach, so it is in your
[backups](./backup.md) with everything else.

**Song files are not sent with your notes** to your other devices, unless the file is under your
notebook's *Send attachments under* limit (see [Backup](./backup.md)). The songs' notes do travel, so
another device lists your songs; add the files there too to play them.

A file is copied when you add it: songs you also keep in a music folder take their space twice.
