# Write, and find it again

## Make something new

Press **＋** to make a note, a board, a discussion, or a note from one of your templates. On a
computer, the **c** key makes a new note straight away.

![The ＋ menu open, offering New note, a whiteboard, a discussion and the other things you can
make](../images/new.png)

**Give it a title that you will search for later.** *Reading list* is a note you will find again;
*Untitled* is not.

## Change a note

A note opens as a page to read. **To change it, click or tap its text**: the editor opens with the
cursor where you touched. To stop, tap the title, press **Escape**, or just leave the note. Nothing
needs saving.

Undid something by mistake? The **↶** button takes back the last thing you did.

## Add a photo, a recording, a file

- **On a phone**, while you write, a row of buttons sits above the keyboard: the **microphone**,
  which writes down what you say where the cursor is; **record**, which keeps the recording in the
  note; and **add a photo or file**.
- **On a computer**, use the note's own **＋ → Add media**: a photo, a video, a recording or any
  file.

To turn a recording into text later, ask Myrme in the note's discussion: `@Myrme /transcribe`.

## Find anything

**Search** looks inside every note, not only at titles, so a phrase you half remember is enough. It
also reads the text of PDFs you have added. On a computer the search field is in the sidebar; on a
phone, it is the magnifier in the bottom bar.

![Search results for "paper": the matching notes, each with its title, first line and
tags](../images/search.png)

Pick a result to open it. More on all of this in
[Creating & organizing notes](../user/notes.md#editing-a-note).
