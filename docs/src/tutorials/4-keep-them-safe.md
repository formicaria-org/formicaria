# Keep them safe

## Your notes are files

Every note is a Markdown file in your notebook's folder. Copying that folder somewhere else is
already a backup, and any text editor can read what is in it.

## Back up

Press **Back up** — the cloud button, near the end of the sidebar, or in the phone's bottom bar. It
records every change, so you can see how a note was last week, and, once your notebook has a place
to go, sends your notes there and brings your other devices up to date.

That place is set up once, in **Settings → Vaults & backup**: another computer, or a private
repository online.

![Settings, Vaults & backup: the notebook's backup repository and what a snapshot would
carry](../images/backup.png)

## Deleted something?

Open the **↶** menu (beside Settings, or in the phone's bottom bar):

- **Undo** takes back the last thing you did, a deletion included.
- **Recently deleted…** lists the notes deleted in the last 30 days, on this device or another one
  that shares the notebook. **Bring back** restores one exactly as it was.

## Nothing leaves without you

Your notes stay on your devices. Nothing is sent anywhere you did not choose: no account, no cloud
of ours. The assistant runs on this device too.

More in [Backup & versioning](../user/backup.md) and
[getting back a deleted note](../user/notes.md#undo-and-getting-back-a-deleted-note).
